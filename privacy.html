Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This app is a private Home Assistant installation used by a single household. It is not available to the public.

What data is accessed

With the account owner's permission, the app reads and can create events in the household's own Google Calendar.

How it is used

Calendar events are shown on dashboards inside the home and used for home automations. The data stays on the household's own Home Assistant server.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's Security settings, under third-party apps with account access.
